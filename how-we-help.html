Our Process

A Proven Process for Predictable Amazon Profit

No guesswork. No vague promises. A structured system that takes you from audit to scale, with every step tracked and reported.

Onboarding: Step by Step

1

Free Store Audit (Days 1–5)

We review your ads, listings, inventory, and margins, then present your biggest profit leaks and opportunities.

2

Strategy Call and Agreement (Days 5–7)

We align on goals, target TACoS, budget, and scope. You choose the plan that fits.

3

Secure Access and Onboarding (Week 1)

You grant limited, role-based access through Amazon’s User Permissions. We never ask for your login credentials. We then set up reporting, tracking, and your Slack channel.

4

Foundation and Quick Wins (Weeks 1–3)

We clean up wasted spend, add negative keywords, restructure campaigns, and fix urgent listing and inventory issues.

5

Growth Build (Days 30–90)

New creative and A+ Content go live, advanced campaigns launch, and replenishment cycles lock in.

6

Scale and Optimize (Day 90+)

With a healthy TACoS, we reinvest profit into new keywords, ASIN expansion, and new marketplaces.

TACoS Monitoring Framework

How We Track the Metric That Matters

1

Set the Baseline

We calculate your current TACoS, break-even ACoS, and target ACoS by product.

2

Segment by Goal

Launch ASINs, defend-rank ASINs, and profit ASINs each get different TACoS targets.

3

Monitor Weekly

TACoS trend lines are reviewed against organic sales growth every week.

4

Diagnose Fast

If TACoS rises while organic sales stay flat, we audit ad waste. If both rise, we protect rank and reinvest.

5

Reinvest Wisely

As TACoS drops, we move budget into high-potential keywords and new products.
