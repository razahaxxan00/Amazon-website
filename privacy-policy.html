Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025 • Effective immediately

1. Overview & Commitment

Aabzone (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is an independent Amazon FBA consulting and account management agency. We take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you browse our website, request a store audit, or contact our team, and how that information is handled.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily submit to us via our Free Store Audit modal, Contact Us form, or direct email correspondence:

  • Contact Details: Full name, business email address, phone number, and WhatsApp handle.
  • Amazon Store Information: Store name, brand name, Amazon store URL, marketplace location (e.g. US, UK, Europe), monthly sales revenue range, advertising budget, and ASINs.
  • Technical Data: Anonymized browser type, device information, and approximate geographical region collected through essential server logs.

⚠ Security Guarantee:

We will NEVER ask for your Amazon Seller Central Master password or bank login. All account access for active clients is granted strictly through Seller Central secondary permissions / user permissions.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following business purposes:

  • Conducting your requested Amazon Store Audit and building your profit roadmap.
  • Replying to your inquiries and scheduling strategic consultations.
  • Providing day-to-day FBA management, PPC optimization, and listing creative services.
  • Maintaining internal business records and complying with statutory obligations.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data or store metrics to any third parties. Data is only processed through trusted infrastructure providers (such as secure email hosts and form processing gateways like Web3Forms / Formspree) bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes minimal, essential cookies and anonymous analytics to monitor page performance, maintain user session preferences, and protect against automated spam. You may decline non-essential cookies via our on-screen cookie notice banner at any time.

6. Data Retention & Security

We employ commercial-grade encryption and access controls to safeguard your data. Audit requests and contact submissions are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill business inquiries or ongoing client management contracts.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable international privacy regulations (including GDPR, UK Data Protection Act, and CCPA), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request complete deletion of your records. To exercise these rights, submit a request via our Contact Form.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to:

Aabzone Agency • Privacy Officer

Inquiries: Submit a message via our Contact Form.
