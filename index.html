The Real Problem

Revenue Is Growing. So Why Isn’t Your Bank Balance?

Most Amazon sellers don’t have a sales problem. They have a profitability problem hiding behind a sales number. Sound familiar?

1

Your ACoS Keeps Climbing

You’re paying for clicks that never convert: broad-match waste, competitor-targeting without a plan, and campaigns nobody has restructured in months. Every wasted click comes straight out of your margin.

2

You Keep Running Out of Stock

One stockout can wipe out months of hard-won keyword rank. Poor forecasting means emergency air freight, lost Buy Box share, and ad spend sent to listings that can’t ship.

3

Your Organic Rank Is Stuck

If every sale depends on paid traffic, you’re renting your growth. Weak listings, thin creative, and no keyword strategy keep you buried on page two.

4

You’re Doing Everything Yourself

PPC, listings, supplier emails, inventory counts, reimbursements. You started a brand, and now you’re running a full-time operations desk.

There’s a better way to run Amazon. It starts with measuring the right number.

Our Core Principle

Built Around One Metric That Actually Matters: TACoS

ACoS tells you how one ad campaign performed. TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sale) tells you how healthy your whole business is. When TACoS falls while revenue grows, your organic rank is compounding and your profit is rising. That is the outcome we engineer.

Profit-First PPC

Every campaign is built against your break-even ACoS and target margin, not an arbitrary sales goal.

Organic Rank Momentum

Smart ad structure plus conversion-focused listings turn paid sales into lasting organic placement.

Stockout Prevention

Demand forecasting and replenishment planning keep your best sellers in stock and your rank intact.

Operational Relief

One accountable team handles ads, listings, inventory, and reporting — so you get your time back.

Radical Transparency

Weekly updates, a live dashboard, and a Slack channel with your team. No black boxes. You always know exactly what we’re doing, why we’re doing it, and what it’s achieving.
