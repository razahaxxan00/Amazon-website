Amazon US Home & Kitchen Full-Service FBA Management

From $3K to $50K/Month: Rebuilding a Bleeding Kitchen Brand

ACoS

45% → 18%

Monthly Revenue

$3k → $50k

Net Margin

4% → 19%

Initial Pain Points

  • ●ACoS stuck at 45% with TACoS of 31%, leaving almost no profit after fees
  • ●Broad-match campaigns burning budget on irrelevant search terms
  • ●Weak main image and thin bullets producing a 7% conversion rate
  • ●Recurring stockouts on the top-selling bundle

Strategic Action Plan

  • ✓Rebuilt ad account into exact, phrase, and discovery structures with strict negative keyword rules
  • ✓Rewrote listing with keyword-mapped titles & bullets, new image stack & A+ Content
  • ✓Set reorder points and a 45-day inventory buffer on top SKUs
  • ✓Shifted budget from low-intent terms to high-converting product-targeting campaigns

90 Days

ACoS 45% → 27%, TACoS 31% → 19%, Revenue $3k → $18k/mo

6 Months

ACoS 18%, TACoS 11%, Revenue $50k/mo, Net margin 4% → 19%

Amazon US Pet Supplies Full Account Takeover

Escaping Stockouts and Reclaiming Cash Flow

Stockouts

3 → Zero

Revenue

$62k → $118k

Founder Time on Ops

20hrs → 3hrs

Initial Pain Points

  • ●Three stockouts in six months, dropping rank by 40% each time
  • ●$38,000 of cash tied up in slow-moving SKUs and long-term storage fees
  • ●Founder spending 20+ hours/week on operations
  • ●Unclear true profit per SKU

Strategic Action Plan

  • ✓Introduced SKU-level profit tracking and weekly inventory dashboard
